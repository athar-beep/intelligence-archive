# Sources & Bibliography

Last reviewed: 5 November 2026

This is a working bibliography. Source type and date are included to help readers judge the scope and evidentiary weight of each publication.

## 1. 3.2.3. Inter-Services Intelligence (ISI)
- **Publisher / author:** European Union Agency for Asylum
- **Date:** October 2026
- **Type:** Country guidance
- **Link:** https://dip-aws.euaa.europa.eu/country-guidance-pakistan/323-inter-services-intelligence-isi
- **Scope note:** Recent institutional overview. The page describes the ISI as a military-run intelligence body and includes reported surveillance and human-rights concerns. Country guidance is a synthesis, not a complete public organisational record.

## 2. 3.1.3. Inter-Services Intelligence (ISI)
- **Publisher / author:** European Union Agency for Asylum
- **Date:** June 2026
- **Type:** Country-of-origin information
- **Link:** https://www.euaa-preprod-aws.euaa.europa.eu/pakistan-country-focus/313-inter-services-intelligence-isi
- **Scope note:** The report states that the ISI was established in 1948 and summarises public descriptions of its foreign-intelligence, counter-intelligence and domestic roles.

## 3. Pakistan-U.S. Relations
- **Publisher / author:** Congressional Research Service; K. Alan Kronstadt
- **Date:** 1 July 2009
- **Type:** Congressional research report
- **Link:** https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/RL33498.html
- **Scope note:** A contemporaneous policy report describing the post-9/11 relationship, intelligence cooperation and longstanding areas of U.S.-Pakistan disagreement. Historical rather than current institutional guidance.

## 4. Pakistan-U.S. Relations
- **Publisher / author:** Congressional Research Service
- **Date:** 24 May 2012
- **Type:** Congressional research report
- **Link:** https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/R41832.html
- **Scope note:** Discusses U.S.-Pakistan intelligence cooperation, including ties dating to the 1980s, and questions raised about the ISI’s role. Check report date and cited references when using a specific passage.

## 5. War in Afghanistan: Strategy, Operations, and Issues for Congress
- **Publisher / author:** Congressional Research Service
- **Date:** 9 March 2011 (updated report)
- **Type:** Congressional research report
- **Link:** https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/R40156.html
- **Scope note:** Places the 1980s Afghan resistance and U.S. assistance through Pakistan’s Inter-Services Intelligence Directorate in a broader historical account.

## 6. Faith, Unity, Discipline: The ISI of Pakistan
- **Publisher / author:** Hein G. Kiessling; Hurst & Company
- **Date:** 2016
- **Type:** Historical monograph
- **Link:** https://hurstpublishers.com/book/faith-unity-discipline/
- **Scope note:** A book-length historical account. The book is a secondary source; its interpretations should be read alongside independent scholarship and primary records.

## 7. Faith, unity, discipline: the ISI of Pakistan
- **Publisher / author:** International Affairs, Oxford University Press; Farzana Shaikh
- **Date:** 1 March 2017
- **Type:** Academic book review
- **Link:** https://academic.oup.com/ia/article-abstract/93/2/504/3056120
- **Scope note:** Bibliographic review of Kiessling’s 2016 historical monograph.

## 8. Pakistan: Security Situation — Country of Origin Information Report
- **Publisher / author:** European Asylum Support Office (now EUAA)
- **Date:** October 2021
- **Type:** Country-of-origin information report
- **Link:** https://www.euaa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/publications/2021_10_EASO_COI_Report_Pakistan_Security_situation.pdf#page=26
- **Scope note:** An older public country report which describes intelligence coordination between military branches. Its cited description is historical public reporting, not a current organisational chart. Relevant discussion appears in the section ‘Inter-Services Intelligence (ISI)’, printed page 26.

## 9. Faith, Unity, Discipline: The Inter-Service-Intelligence (ISI)
- **Publisher / author:** Institute of Peace and Conflict Studies
- **Date:** 2017
- **Type:** Research institute commentary
- **Link:** https://ipcs.org/comm_select.php?articleNo=5226
- **Scope note:** A discussion by Hein G. Kiessling of his historical work, including the agency’s 1948 founding and the 1971 loss of East Pakistan. It is an author’s account, not a primary document.

## 10. Country of Origin Information: Pakistan — Country Focus
- **Publisher / author:** European Union Agency for Asylum
- **Date:** December 2024
- **Type:** Country-of-origin information
- **Link:** https://www.euaa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/publications/2024-12/2024_12_EUAA_COI_Report_Pakistan_Country_Focus.pdf
- **Scope note:** Broader political and security context. Use the report’s numbered footnotes and original sources for detailed claims.
